QA — Arithmetic Speed Drill

20 questions · 25 minutes · 75 seconds each

Arithmetic is ~45% of the QA section and the fastest-scoring content in the entire exam. None of these is conceptually hard. The whole exercise is speed and clean execution.

Rules: - Time yourself. 25 minutes, hard stop. - No written long division or long multiplication. Force yourself into ratios, percentages-as-fractions, and approximation. - Mark any question that took you over 90 seconds. Those are your real targets.

Target: 18+ correct in under 25 minutes. If you're below that, the fix is reps, not theory.

Questions

1. If 40% of a number is 56, what is 65% of the same number?

2. A price rises by 25% and then falls by 20%. What is the net percentage change?

3. A sum of money triples in 20 years at simple interest. What is the annual rate?

4. Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. When 4 is added to each, the ratio becomes 2 : 3. Find the larger number.

5. The average of 10 numbers is 45. One of them, 63, is removed. What is the average of the remaining nine?

6. A can complete a job in 15 days and B in 10 days. They work on alternate days, A starting. How many days does the job take?

7. A 180-metre-long train crosses a pole in 12 seconds. What is its speed in km/h?

8. A 200-metre-long train travelling at 72 km/h crosses a platform in 20 seconds. How long is the platform?

9. Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7 and their LCM is 210. What is the smaller number?

10. Fifteen men complete a piece of work in 20 days. How many days would 25 men take?

11. The cost price of 20 articles equals the selling price of 16 articles. What is the profit percentage?

12. A number increased by 20% becomes 1200. What was the original number?

13. Successive discounts of 20% and 25% are equivalent to a single discount of what percentage?

14. A 40-litre mixture has milk and water in the ratio 3 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 3 : 2?

15. Find the simple interest on ₹8,000 for 3 years at 12% per annum.

16. Find the compound interest on ₹10,000 for 2 years at 10% per annum.

17. A car covers the first half of a journey at 40 km/h and the second half at 60 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole journey?

18. If 12 : 18 :: 20 : x, find x.

19. A shopkeeper sells an article for ₹270 at a 10% loss. At what price should he sell it to make a 20% profit?

20. Pipes A and B can fill a tank in 12 and 18 minutes respectively, while pipe C can empty it in 9 minutes. If all three are opened together, how long does the tank take to fill?

---

SOLUTIONS

1. 91. 40% = 56 → the number is 140. 65% of 140 = 91. Don't compute the number if you can avoid it: 65/40 × 56 = 13/8 × 56 = 13 × 7 = 91.

2. 0%. 1.25 × 0.80 = 1.00. Exactly no change. Worth burning in: +25% then −20% cancels, as does +20%/−16⅔%, +50%/−33⅓%, +100%/−50%. The pattern is +1/n then −1/(n+1).

3. 10%. Tripling means interest = 2P over 20 years. 2P = P × r × 20/100 → r = 10%.

4. 20. (3x + 4)/(5x + 4) = 2/3 → 9x + 12 = 10x + 8 → x = 4. Numbers are 12 and 20, so the larger is 20.

5. 43. Total = 450. Remove 63 → 387. 387/9 = 43. Faster: 63 is 18 above the old average, and removing it spreads that −18 over 9 numbers, i.e. −2. So 45 − 2 = 43.

6. 12 days. Rates 1/15 and 1/10. A two-day block does 1/15 + 1/10 = 1/6. Six blocks = 12 days, completing exactly 1. 12 days. Watch for the variant where the work finishes mid-block — always check whether the last partial day is enough.

7. 54 km/h. 180/12 = 15 m/s. × 18/5 = 54 km/h. m/s → km/h is × 18/5; km/h → m/s is × 5/18. You should not have to think about this.

8. 200 m. 72 km/h = 20 m/s. In 20 s it covers 400 m total = train + platform. Platform = 400 − 200 = 200 m.

9. 30. Numbers are 5k and 7k with k as the HCF, so LCM = 35k = 210 → k = 6. Smaller = 30.

10. 12 days. Total work = 15 × 20 = 300 man-days. 300/25 = 12.

11. 25%. 20C = 16S → S/C = 5/4 → profit = 25%. General form: if CP of m articles = SP of n articles, profit% = (m − n)/n × 100.

12. 1000. 1.2x = 1200 → x = 1000.

13. 40%. 0.80 × 0.75 = 0.60, so 40% off. Or use a + b − ab/100 = 20 + 25 − 5 = 40.

14. 10 litres. Milk = 30, water = 10. Milk doesn't change, so for 3 : 2 with milk at 30, water must be 20. Add 10 litres.

15. ₹2,880. 8000 × 0.12 × 3 = 2880.

16. ₹2,100. 10000 × 1.1² = 12100, so CI = 2100. Or: SI for 2 years = 2000, plus interest-on-interest of 10% of 1000 = 100. Total 2100. This decomposition is much faster for 2-year and 3-year CI.

17. 48 km/h. Equal distances, so use the harmonic mean: 2(40)(60)/(40 + 60) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h. Equal distances → harmonic mean. Equal times → arithmetic mean. Mixing these up is one of the most common CAT errors.

18. 30. 12/18 = 2/3, so x = 20 × 3/2 = 30.

19. ₹360. 0.9 × CP = 270 → CP = 300. For 20% profit: 1.2 × 300 = ₹360.

20. 36 minutes. Net rate = 1/12 + 1/18 − 1/9 = 3/36 + 2/36 − 4/36 = 1/36. So 36 minutes. Always take an LCM for the denominators rather than working in decimals — here LCM(12,18,9) = 36 gives the answer directly.

What to fix

Log which questions took you over 90 seconds. The usual culprits and their fixes:

If you were slow on The fix
1, 12, 13 Percentage-as-fraction fluency. 1/7 = 14.28%, 1/8 = 12.5%, 1/9 = 11.11%, 1/11 = 9.09%, 1/12 = 8.33%, 1/13 = 7.69%, 1/16 = 6.25%. Memorise.
6, 10, 20 Work problems — always convert to a common "total units of work" (use the LCM of the times) rather than fractions.
7, 8, 17 Unit conversion reflex and the harmonic/arithmetic mean distinction.
2, 13 Successive-change shortcuts.
16 The SI-plus-interest-on-interest decomposition for CI.
