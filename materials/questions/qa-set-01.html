QA — Sectional Simulation #1

22 questions · 40 minutes · +3 / −1 (MCQ), +3 / 0 (TITA)

Run this exactly like the real section. Three passes (see 03-playbook.md). No calculator. Rough sheet and pen only.

The point of this set is not your score. It is to practise abandoning questions. Q17, Q20 and Q22 are deliberate time sinks. A good run leaves 4–5 questions untouched.

Solutions at the bottom. Don't scroll.

Questions

1. A shopkeeper uses a weight of 900 g while claiming to sell one kilogram. He also marks his goods 25% above cost and then offers a 10% discount on the marked price. His overall profit percentage is:

  (a) 12.5%    (b) 15%    (c) 22.5%    (d) 25%

2. (TITA) Two trains leave stations A and B simultaneously, travelling towards each other, and meet after 6 hours. The train that started from A then takes 4 more hours to reach B. How many more hours after the meeting does the train from B take to reach A?

3. A and B together can finish a job in 12 days. They work together for 5 days, after which A leaves and B completes the remaining work in 21 days. How many days would A alone take to finish the whole job?

  (a) 15    (b) 18    (c) 20    (d) 24

4. (TITA) A vessel contains 80 litres of a milk–water mixture in the ratio 5 : 3. Some of the mixture is drawn out and replaced by an equal quantity of pure water, after which milk and water are in the ratio 1 : 1. How many litres of the mixture were drawn out?

5. (TITA) The average of five distinct positive integers is 20. If the largest of them is 40, what is the maximum possible value of the second largest?

6. If both roots of the equation x² − px + q = 0 are consecutive integers, which of the following must be true?

  (a) p² − 4q = 1    (b) p² = 4q    (c) p² − 4q = 2    (d) p = q

7. (TITA) If log₂x + log₄x + log₁₆x = 7, find x.

8. (TITA) How many integer values of x satisfy |x − 3| + |x + 2| ≤ 9?

9. (TITA) What is the remainder when 7¹⁰³ is divided by 100?

10. (TITA) How many factors of 2⁵ × 3⁶ × 5² are perfect squares?

11. In triangle ABC, AB = 13, BC = 14 and CA = 15. What is the length of the altitude drawn from A to BC?

  (a) 11    (b) 12    (c) 12.5    (d) 13

12. A circle is inscribed in a right-angled triangle whose legs measure 6 and 8. The radius of the circle is:

  (a) 1.5    (b) 2    (c) 2.4    (d) 3

13. (TITA) A solid cylinder of radius 3 cm and height 8 cm is melted and recast into solid spheres of radius 1 cm. How many complete spheres can be made?

14. (TITA) In how many ways can the letters of the word PERMUTE be arranged so that no two vowels are adjacent?

15. Two fair dice are rolled. Given that the sum of the numbers shown is even, what is the probability that both dice show the same number?

  (a) 1/6    (b) 1/4    (c) 1/3    (d) 1/2

16. (TITA) The sum of the first n terms of an arithmetic progression is 3n² + 5n. What is its 10th term?

17. Let f(x) = x/(x − 1) for x ≠ 1. Then f(f(f(x))) equals:

  (a) x    (b) x/(x − 1)    (c) (x − 1)/x    (d) 1/(1 − x)

18. (TITA) A boat travels 30 km downstream and returns 30 km upstream in a total of 8 hours. If the speed of the stream is 2 km/h, what is the speed of the boat in still water, in km/h?

19. A sum of money invested at compound interest doubles in 8 years. In how many years will it become eight times itself?

  (a) 16    (b) 20    (c) 24    (d) 32

20. (TITA) What is the area of the region in the coordinate plane satisfying both |x| + |y| ≤ 4 and x ≥ 0?

21. In a class, the ratio of boys to girls is 3 : 2. The average score of the boys is 68 and the average score of the whole class is 72. What is the average score of the girls?

  (a) 74    (b) 76    (c) 78    (d) 80

22. (TITA) How many ordered pairs of positive integers (a, b) satisfy 1/a + 1/b = 1/12?

SOLUTIONS

1. (d) 25%. Take cost = ₹1/g. Customer pays the price of 1 kg: MP = 1.25 × 1000 = 1250, SP = 0.9 × 1250 = 1125. Goods actually handed over = 900 g, costing 900. Profit = 225/900 = 25%. The trick is that the two cheats compound on different bases — the weight cheat on quantity, the markup/discount on price. Don't add 25% and 12.5%.

2. 9. Let the speeds be u (from A) and v (from B). Before meeting, the A-train covers 6u and has 6v left, which takes 6v/u = 4, so v/u = 2/3. The B-train has 6u left, taking 6u/v = 6/(2/3) = 9 hours. Worth memorising the shortcut: if they meet after t hours and then take a and b hours respectively, t = √(ab). Here 6 = √(4b) → b = 9. Turns a 90-second question into a 10-second one.

3. (b) 18. Combined rate = 1/12. In 5 days, 5/12 is done, leaving 7/12. B does 7/12 in 21 days, so B's rate = 1/36. A's rate = 1/12 − 1/36 = 1/18. A alone takes 18 days.

4. 16. Initially milk = 50, water = 30, total 80 (which stays 80). Final ratio 1 : 1 means milk = 40. Drawing out x litres removes (5/8)x of milk: 50 − 5x/8 = 40 → x = 16 litres.

5. 39. Sum = 100. With the largest at 40, the second largest is at most 39 (distinct). Check 39 works: the other three must be distinct positive integers summing to 100 − 40 − 39 = 21, e.g. 1 + 2 + 18. So 39. Classic maximise-one-by-minimising-the-rest, but you must verify feasibility. Blindly minimising the bottom three to 1, 2, 3 gives 54, which violates "largest = 40."

6. (a) p² − 4q = 1. Roots n and n+1 give p = 2n + 1 and q = n(n+1). Then p² − 4q = (4n² + 4n + 1) − (4n² + 4n) = 1. Faster: p² − 4q is the discriminant, which equals (difference of roots)² = 1² = 1. Two seconds if you see it.

7. 16. log₄x = ½·log₂x and log₁₆x = ¼·log₂x. With t = log₂x: t(1 + ½ + ¼) = 7 → (7/4)t = 7 → t = 4 → x = 16.

8. 10. The expression is the sum of distances from 3 and from −2, minimum 5 on the interval [−2, 3]. For x > 3: 2x − 1 ≤ 9 → x ≤ 5. For x < −2: 1 − 2x ≤ 9 → x ≥ −4. So x ∈ [−4, 5], giving 10 integers.

9. 43. 7¹ = 7, 7² = 49, 7³ ≡ 43, 7⁴ ≡ 1 (mod 100). Cycle length 4. 103 = 4(25) + 3, so 7¹⁰³ ≡ 7³ ≡ 43.

10. 24. A perfect-square factor needs all exponents even. For 2⁵: {0, 2, 4} = 3 choices. For 3⁶: {0, 2, 4, 6} = 4. For 5²: {0, 2} = 2. Total 3 × 4 × 2 = 24.

11. (b) 12. s = 21, so area = √(21·8·7·6) = √7056 = 84. Then 84 = ½ × 14 × h → h = 12. 13-14-15 is a standard CAT triangle (two 3-4-5-family right triangles glued along the altitude of 12). Recognise it and skip Heron entirely.

12. (b) 2. For a right triangle, r = (a + b − c)/2 = (6 + 8 − 10)/2 = 2.

13. 54. Cylinder volume = π(9)(8) = 72π. Sphere volume = (4/3)π. 72π ÷ (4π/3) = 54.

14. 720. PERMUTE has 7 letters: consonants P, R, M, T (4 distinct) and vowels E, U, E (3, with E repeated). Arrange the consonants: 4! = 24. This creates 5 gaps; choose 3 and place the vowels one per gap: C(5,3) × 3!/2! = 10 × 3 = 30. Total = 24 × 30 = 720. The repeated E is the trap — dividing by 2! at the wrong stage is the standard error.

15. (c) 1/3. Sum is even when both are odd (3 × 3 = 9 ways) or both even (9 ways) = 18 outcomes. Doubles number 6, and every double has an even sum. P = 6/18 = 1/3.

16. 62. aₙ = Sₙ − Sₙ₋₁ = (3n² + 5n) − (3(n−1)² + 5(n−1)) = 6n + 2. So a₁₀ = 62.

17. (b) x/(x − 1). f(f(x)) = [x/(x−1)] ÷ [x/(x−1) − 1] = [x/(x−1)] ÷ [1/(x−1)] = x. So f is its own inverse, f² is the identity, and f³ = f = x/(x − 1). Spotting f² = identity turns this into a one-liner. Alternatively plug x = 2: f(2) = 2, so f³(2) = 2 — which rules out (c) and (d) but not (a) or (b). Plug x = 3: f(3) = 1.5, f(1.5) = 3, f(3) = 1.5. Option (a) gives 3, option (b) gives 1.5. Answer (b).

18. 8. 30/(b+2) + 30/(b−2) = 8 → 30(2b) = 8(b² − 4) → 2b² − 15b − 8 = 0 → b = (15 + 17)/4 = 8 km/h. Faster: just try the sensible integers. b = 8 gives 30/10 + 30/6 = 3 + 5 = 8. Done in 15 seconds.

19. (c) 24. Doubling takes 8 years, and 8 = 2³, so three doublings: 24 years. (Compound growth is multiplicative — do not scale linearly.)

20. 16. |x| + |y| ≤ 4 is a square rotated 45°, with both diagonals of length 8, so area = ½ × 8 × 8 = 32. The condition x ≥ 0 takes exactly half of it by symmetry: 16.

21. (c) 78. 72 × 5 = 3 × 68 + 2g → 360 = 204 + 2g → g = 78. Alligation is faster: boys are 4 below the mean, and the boy : girl ratio is 3 : 2, so girls are 3/2 × 4 = 6 above the mean. 72 + 6 = 78.

22. 15. Rearranging: ab − 12a − 12b = 0 → (a − 12)(b − 12) = 144. Both factors must be positive (if both were negative, a and b would be under 12, and the largest possible product is 11 × 11 = 121 < 144). So the count equals the number of divisors of 144 = 2⁴ × 3²: (4+1)(2+1) = 15 ordered pairs.

Post-mortem

For each miss, tag it KNOWLEDGE / SLOW / CARELESS / SELECTION / BLIND and log the counts in tracker.md.

For this set specifically, the questions worth not attempting on a first pass were 14 (P&C with a repeat — slow and error-prone), 20 (geometry you have to visualise) and 22 (needs the factoring insight). If you took all three and ran out of time for 1, 3 and 21 — which are 60-second arithmetic questions — that is a selection failure, not a maths failure, and it's the single most important thing this set can teach you.
